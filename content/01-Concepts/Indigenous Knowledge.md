# Indigenous Knowledge

> Knowledge systems developed by Indigenous peoples through long-standing relationships with lands, cultures, and communities. Indigenous Knowledge requires specific governance protections—including CARE principles and data sovereignty frameworks—against AI systems that may extract, decontextualize, or misrepresent cultural knowledge without community consent or benefit.
