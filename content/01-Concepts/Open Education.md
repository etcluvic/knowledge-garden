# Open Education

> Educational practices and resources designed to be freely accessible, adaptable, and shareable. AI intersects with open education through tools that can democratize access to learning while also raising concerns about academic integrity, the erosion of cognitive development through task offloading, and equity in access to AI-enhanced learning.
