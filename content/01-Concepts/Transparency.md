# Transparency

> The principle that AI systems, their development processes, and their impacts should be open to inspection and understanding. Transparency encompasses model documentation, data provenance, algorithmic decision-making, and institutional governance practices—enabling scrutiny, contestation, and informed participation in AI-mediated scholarship.
