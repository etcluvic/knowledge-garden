# Fair Principles

> The FAIR framework—Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable—provides technical standards for data stewardship that enable effective sharing and reuse. FAIR principles establish baseline requirements for data infrastructure, while complementary frameworks like CARE add dimensions of community benefit and ethical governance.
