# Data Sovereignty

> The right of communities—particularly Indigenous peoples—to govern the collection, ownership, and use of data about them. Data sovereignty challenges the assumption that data should be freely accessible regardless of context, asserting collective rights over cultural knowledge and community information against extractive data practices.
