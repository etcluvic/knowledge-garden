# Accessibility

> The principle that knowledge, tools, and platforms should be usable by all people regardless of language, ability, technical literacy, or geographic location. In AI-mediated scholarship, accessibility extends beyond physical access to encompass linguistic, economic, and infrastructural barriers that shape unequal participation. True accessibility requires multidimensional attention to the diverse ways people engage with knowledge.
