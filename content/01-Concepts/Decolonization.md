# Decolonization

> Critical approaches that challenge colonial power structures embedded in knowledge systems, technologies, and institutions. In AI contexts, decolonization addresses how training data, evaluation metrics, and design assumptions reproduce colonial patterns of extraction, erasure, and the subordination of non-Western knowledge traditions.
