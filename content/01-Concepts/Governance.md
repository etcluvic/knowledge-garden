# Governance

> The structures, processes, and norms through which decisions about AI development and deployment are made and enforced. Governance encompasses policy, regulation, institutional frameworks, community standards, and technical mechanisms, requiring adaptive approaches that can respond to emergent properties of complex sociotechnical systems.
