# Research Integrity

> The principles and practices ensuring trustworthiness of the scholarly record. AI amplifies threats to research integrity through paper mills, fabricated reviews, synthetic citations, and AI-generated manuscripts—while also offering tools for detection, requiring comprehensive governance approaches that address both defensive and constructive dimensions.
