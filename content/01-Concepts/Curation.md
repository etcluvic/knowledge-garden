# Curation

> The selection, organization, and presentation of knowledge resources, increasingly mediated by algorithmic systems. AI-powered curation through recommendation engines, search ranking, and content moderation shapes what knowledge becomes visible and accessible, influencing canon formation and the distribution of scholarly attention.
