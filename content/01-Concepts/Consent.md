# Consent

> The principle that individuals and communities should have meaningful say in how their data, knowledge, and intellectual contributions are collected, used, and shared. Consent becomes particularly complex in AI contexts where training data is harvested at scale and repurposed in ways creators may not have anticipated or authorized.
