# Intellectual Property

> Legal frameworks governing ownership and use of creative and intellectual works. AI challenges intellectual property regimes by consuming copyrighted works for training, generating derivative outputs, and blurring traditional boundaries of authorship—necessitating new approaches to licensing, attribution, and benefit-sharing.
