# Epistemic Justice

> The equitable distribution of credibility, recognition, and participation in knowledge-making processes. Epistemic justice addresses how AI systems can perpetuate epistemic harms—including testimonial injustice (dismissing knowers) and hermeneutical injustice (excluding interpretive frameworks)—particularly for marginalized communities.
