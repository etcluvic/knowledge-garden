# Explainability

> The capacity to understand and articulate how AI systems produce their outputs. Explainability is crucial for scholarly accountability—researchers, reviewers, and publics need to understand AI-mediated processes to maintain trust, enable contestation, and ensure that consequential decisions can be scrutinized and justified.
