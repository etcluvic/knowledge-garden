# Metadata

> Structured information describing, organizing, and enabling discovery of knowledge resources. Metadata shapes what gets found, how work is categorized, and whose contributions become visible—making metadata standards and practices crucial infrastructure for equitable scholarly communication in AI-mediated environments.
