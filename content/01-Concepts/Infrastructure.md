# Infrastructure

> The technical, institutional, and social systems that enable knowledge production, circulation, and preservation. AI is transforming scholarly infrastructure through platformization, algorithmic mediation, and the integration of automated functions into repositories, journals, and communication systems—raising questions about governance, sustainability, and public accountability.
