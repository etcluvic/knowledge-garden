# Interoperability

> The ability of different systems, platforms, and data formats to work together effectively. Interoperability is essential for open scholarly infrastructure, enabling knowledge to flow across repositories, journals, and tools while maintaining provenance and context—and is increasingly important as AI mediates connections between diverse systems.
