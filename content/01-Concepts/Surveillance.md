# Surveillance

> The systematic monitoring and collection of data about individuals and communities, often for commercial or governmental purposes. In scholarly contexts, surveillance publishing describes how publishers monetize researcher behavior alongside research outputs, extending into AI domains where behavioral data feeds predictive models.
