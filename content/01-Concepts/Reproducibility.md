# Reproducibility

> The ability to independently verify research findings by replicating methods and analyses. AI introduces challenges to reproducibility through opaque model architectures, proprietary training data, and non-deterministic outputs—making documentation standards, model cards, and open benchmarking essential for maintaining scientific rigor.
