# Enclosure

> The process by which shared resources are captured for private benefit, restricting access that was previously open. In AI, enclosure describes how open access literature, community-built datasets, and public knowledge are absorbed into proprietary systems that generate commercial value without meaningful reciprocity to contributing communities.
