# Large Language Models

> AI systems trained on massive text corpora to generate and process language. LLMs inherit connectionist traditions of statistical pattern recognition, excelling at fluent text generation while remaining weak at grounding, verification, and accountable judgment—making their role in scholarship both promising and perilous.
