# Hallucination

> False outputs generated by AI systems that appear plausible due to their fluent and confident surface presentation. Hallucinations stem from the mathematical design of generative models optimized to produce probable outputs rather than verify claims, posing particular risks for scholarship where accuracy and evidence-grounding are essential.
