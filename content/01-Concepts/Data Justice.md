# Data Justice

> A framework for evaluating the fairness of data practices, encompassing who collects data, how it is used, who benefits, and who bears harms. Data justice extends beyond individual privacy to address structural inequalities in data ecosystems, including the colonial dynamics of data extraction from marginalized communities.
