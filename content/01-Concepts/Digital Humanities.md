# Digital Humanities

> An interdisciplinary field combining computational methods with humanistic inquiry. Digital Humanities provides critical frameworks for understanding AI as a sociotechnical formation, foregrounding how datasets, labels, and evaluation metrics encode values and worldviews that shape knowledge production.
