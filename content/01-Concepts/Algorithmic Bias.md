# Algorithmic Bias

> Systematic patterns of unfairness in algorithmic outputs that emerge from biased training data, flawed model design, or societal inequities encoded in technical systems. Algorithmic bias operates across data collection, method selection, and deployment contexts, often reproducing and amplifying existing social inequalities in ways that disproportionately affect marginalized communities.
