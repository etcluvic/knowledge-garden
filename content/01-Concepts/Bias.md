# Bias

> Systematic distortions in AI systems that produce unfair outcomes, emerging from training data, algorithmic design, societal structures, or the interaction of all three. Bias in AI is not merely a technical problem solvable through debiasing techniques; it reflects deeper ideological commitments embedded in data collection, labeling practices, and evaluation metrics.
