# Peer Review

> The scholarly process of subjecting research to evaluation by disciplinary experts. AI is transforming peer review through tools for triage, formatting checks, and detection tasks, while human judgment remains essential for assessments of novelty, significance, and methodological rigor—creating a contested boundary between operational assistance and epistemic authority.
