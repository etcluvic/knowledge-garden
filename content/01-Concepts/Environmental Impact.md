# Environmental Impact

> The ecological costs of AI systems, including energy consumption for training and deployment, water usage for cooling, and electronic waste. Environmental impacts intersect with justice concerns as communities bearing disproportionate environmental burdens are often those least likely to benefit from AI capabilities.
