# Trust

> The relational foundation enabling cooperation, knowledge exchange, and collective inquiry. AI mediates trust through both constructive functions (verification, provenance tracking) and destructive ones (synthetic content, deepfakes, hallucinations)—making trust infrastructure a critical concern for scholarly communities navigating AI-mediated environments.
