# Scholarly Communication

> The systems and practices through which research is created, evaluated, disseminated, and preserved. AI is restructuring scholarly communication through automated discovery, algorithmic recommendation, AI-assisted writing and review, and platform-mediated engagement—challenging established norms of authorship, attribution, and quality assurance.
