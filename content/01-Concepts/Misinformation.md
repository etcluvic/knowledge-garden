# Misinformation

> False or misleading information, amplified by AI's capacity to generate plausible-sounding content at scale. AI-generated misinformation poses particular challenges for scholarship and public trust, as the fluency of synthetic text makes discrimination between reliable and unreliable claims increasingly difficult.
