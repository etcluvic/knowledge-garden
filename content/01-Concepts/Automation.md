# Automation

> The delegation of tasks previously requiring human judgment to computational systems. In scholarly contexts, automation redistributes discretion and authority—tasks once requiring situated human assessment are handled by systems lacking context. Automation theory highlights how this redistribution creates new questions about where human oversight must remain central.
