# Knowledge Production

> The processes through which claims, evidence, and understanding are generated, validated, and circulated. AI transforms knowledge production by introducing new modes of synthesis, generation, and discovery while challenging established processes of peer review, deliberation, and community validation that give scholarship its epistemic authority.
