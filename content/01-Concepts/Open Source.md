# Open Source

> Software, models, and tools whose source code and development artifacts are freely available for inspection, modification, and redistribution. In AI, open source dynamics encompass open weights, community checkpoints, and graduated transparency—with ongoing debates about what constitutes genuine openness versus 'openwashing.'
