# Open Data

> The practice of making research data freely available for use, reuse, and redistribution. Open data is essential for reproducibility and model improvement, but raises issues of privacy, consent, and community sovereignty—requiring frameworks that balance openness with protections for sensitive, personal, and Indigenous data.
