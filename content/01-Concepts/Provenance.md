# Provenance

> The documented history of a knowledge artifact's origin, transformation, and chain of custody. Provenance becomes critical in AI-mediated scholarship where content credentials, persistent identifiers, and metadata trails enable traceability and accountability—supporting trust in an environment where synthetic and human-authored content coexist.
