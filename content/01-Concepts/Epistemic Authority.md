# Epistemic Authority

> The recognized capacity to produce, validate, and certify knowledge claims. AI challenges epistemic authority by performing tasks traditionally associated with expert judgment—summarizing literature, generating analyses, assisting peer review—while lacking the contextual understanding, accountability, and community embeddedness that ground legitimate knowledge production.
