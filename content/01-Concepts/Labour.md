# Labour

> The human work—often invisible and precarious—that underpins AI capabilities. Labour in AI encompasses annotation, moderation, data labeling, and content review performed disproportionately by workers in the Global South under poor conditions, connecting AI development to broader patterns of extraction and exploitation.
