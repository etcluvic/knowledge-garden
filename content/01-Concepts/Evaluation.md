# Evaluation

> The processes through which scholarly work, AI systems, and their impacts are assessed. AI disrupts traditional evaluation by introducing new questions: How should AI-assisted work be judged? What metrics capture social impact alongside technical performance? How can evaluation remain attentive to differential effects across communities?
