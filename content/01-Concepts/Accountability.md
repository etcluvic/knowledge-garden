# Accountability

> The requirement that individuals, institutions, and systems bear responsibility for their decisions and impacts. In AI contexts, accountability addresses who is answerable when automated systems produce errors, biases, or harms—particularly given the opacity of many AI systems and the diffusion of decision-making across human-machine assemblages.
