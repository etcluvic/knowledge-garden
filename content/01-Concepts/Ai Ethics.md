# Ai Ethics

> The interdisciplinary field examining moral dimensions of AI design, deployment, and governance. AI ethics addresses questions of fairness, transparency, consent, harm prevention, and human dignity, drawing on philosophy, law, social science, and community knowledge to establish normative frameworks for responsible AI development.
