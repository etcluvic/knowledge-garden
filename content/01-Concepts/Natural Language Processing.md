# Natural Language Processing

> AI techniques for understanding, generating, and manipulating human language. NLP has evolved from rule-based symbolic approaches through statistical methods to contemporary transformer-based models, each transition carrying implications for what aspects of language and knowledge are privileged or marginalized.
