# Commons

> Shared resources—including knowledge, data, software, and infrastructure—governed collectively for public benefit rather than private extraction. The scholarly commons encompasses open access literature, open datasets, community-maintained software, and collaborative infrastructures whose value is built through distributed collective labor over decades.
