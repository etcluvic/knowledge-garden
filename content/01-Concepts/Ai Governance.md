# Ai Governance

> The institutional frameworks, policies, processes, and norms through which organizations and societies manage AI development and deployment. AI governance spans regulatory approaches, organizational policies, community standards, and technical safeguards, requiring adaptive structures that can evolve alongside rapidly changing technologies.
