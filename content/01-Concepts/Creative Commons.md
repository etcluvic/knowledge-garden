# Creative Commons

> A licensing system that enables creators to grant permissions for use, sharing, and adaptation of their work under specified conditions. In AI contexts, Creative Commons licenses face new challenges as works licensed for human access are repurposed for machine training, raising questions about whether existing licenses adequately govern machine consumption.
