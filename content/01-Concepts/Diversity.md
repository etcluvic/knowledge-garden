# Diversity

> The multiplicity of perspectives, languages, methods, and knowledge traditions that constitute robust scholarship. In AI contexts, diversity concerns encompass representation in training data, inclusion of diverse communities in AI governance, and the preservation of epistemic pluralism against homogenizing tendencies of large-scale models.
