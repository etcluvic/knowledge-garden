## Cross-Cutting Tensions

These four tensions structure AI's engagement with scholarship:

```
    Operational Assistance ←──→ Epistemic Authority
    Openness              ←──→ Enclosure
    Technical Capability  ←──→ Organizational Capacity
    Efficiency            ←──→ Process
```

Each tension is a **site of institutional choice**, not a problem to be solved.
