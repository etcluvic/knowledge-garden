# 🧭 Taking Bearings — AI and Open Knowledge Production

> *A knowledge garden mapping the landscape where artificial intelligence meets open scholarship, knowledge production, and social implications — built from 180 research sources and structured for exploration.*

---

## 🌱 What Is This?

This is a **knowledge garden** — an interconnected web of notes, concepts, and annotations drawn from the research scan *AI and Open Knowledge Production*. Unlike a traditional paper or report, this garden is designed to be **explored non-linearly**: follow links, browse by theme, or trace connections across the network.

**Who is it for?** Researchers, practitioners, policymakers, and anyone interested in how AI is reshaping open scholarship and knowledge production.

**What does it contain?**

| What | Count | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 📄 Annotations | 180 | Summarized research sources with metadata and cross-references |
| 💡 Concept Hubs | 55 | Key ideas linking related annotations together |
| ⚡ Tensions | 4 | Cross-cutting debates that run through the literature |
| 🌐 Domains | 4 | Major thematic areas organizing the research |
| 🔗 Cross-References | 593 | Curated connections between notes (top 3 per annotation) |

This knowledge garden was generated from the research scan *"Taking Bearings: AI and Open Knowledge Production"* through NLP-enhanced content analysis and semantic linking. It is designed to be published with [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/) as a static website and can also be explored locally in [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/).

