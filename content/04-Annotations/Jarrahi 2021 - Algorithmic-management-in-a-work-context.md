# Algorithmic management in a work context

**Jarrahi, M. H., Newlands, G., Lee, M. K., Wolf, C. T., Kinder, E; Sutherland, W** (2021)

*Big Data & Society, 8(2), 20539517211020332*

🔗 [https://doi.org/10.1177/20539517211020332](https://doi.org/10.1177/20539517211020332)

## Annotation

Analyzes algorithmic management as a sociotechnical phenomenon reshaping power, discretion, and information flows in organizations; details opacity at technical and organizational levels. Exposes determinist narratives in “smart” automation of management decisions; shows how design choices redistribute authority and —central to a nondeterministic, governance-forward stance.
